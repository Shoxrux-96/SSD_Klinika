# Professional klinika tizimi roadmapi

## 1. Maqsad
Bu loyiha oddiy klinika CRM dan professional klinika ERP / EMR tizimiga o'tishini ta’minlashdir.

## 2. Asosiy modullar
- Bemor EMR / EHR
- Treatment plan
- Billing and finance
- Inventory and stock
- Reports and KPI
- Notifications and reminders
- Lab integration
- PDF document automation

## 3. Rol bo‘yicha dashboardlar
### Admin
- KPI overview
- Operation monitoring
- Clinical metrics
- Finance and debts
- Inventory
- Alerts and security audit

### Shifokor
- Bugungi bemorlar
- Bemor EMR
- Treatment plan
- Lab results
- Doctor notes and prescriptions

### Laborant
- Lab queue
- Result entry
- PDF export
- Status update

### Registrator
- Appointment board
- New patient intake
- Follow-up and reminders
- Payment status

### Kassir
- Cash box
- Invoices and receipts
- Debts
- Daily report

### Buxgalter
- Finance summary
- Inventory
- Payroll
- Financial reports

## 4. Implementatsiya ketma-ketligi
1. EMR / patient profile
2. Appointments and doctor schedule
3. Treatment plan and diagnosis workflow
4. Billing and payment pipeline
5. Reports and KPI board
6. Inventory
7. Lab integration
8. Notifications and reminders
9. PDF document automation
10. Security, audit and backup

## 5. Ruxsatlar tamoyili
- Minimal necessary access
- Backend RBAC final authority
- Role-specific dashboard
- Audit logs for every sensitive change
- Production auth must remain enabled

## 6. Xulosa
Bu tizim professional bo‘lishi uchun faqat CRUD emas, balki klinika operatsion jarayoni, moliya, hisobot, lab, hujjat va eslatmalar oqimlari birga ishlashi kerak.
